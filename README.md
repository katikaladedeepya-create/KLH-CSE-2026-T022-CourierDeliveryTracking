## Abstract

The **Courier Delivery and Tracking System** is a software application designed to simplify the process of courier booking, delivery management, and shipment tracking. The system allows customers to create courier requests and track their shipments using a unique tracking ID.

The system maintains courier details such as sender information, receiver information, pickup location, destination, courier status, and estimated delivery details. It also helps delivery personnel and administrators manage shipments and update their delivery status.

The main objective of this project is to provide a simple, efficient, and transparent platform for managing courier deliveries and allowing customers to monitor their shipments in real time.

---

## Objectives

* To provide an easy way to book courier deliveries.
* To generate a unique tracking ID for every courier.
* To allow customers to track their courier status.
* To maintain sender and receiver information.
* To update courier status throughout the delivery process.
* To help administrators manage courier records.
* To reduce manual work involved in courier management.
* To provide a centralized system for storing courier information.
